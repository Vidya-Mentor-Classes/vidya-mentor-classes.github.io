How to install

Since this isn't from the Play Store, Android shows one extra step — it only takes a minute.

1

Download App Now

Tap the button above — the APK file will start downloading.

2

Install anyway

Android may show a warning since this isn't from the Play Store. Tap "Install anyway" to continue.

3

Click Downloads

Open your phone's Downloads folder, or tap the download notification.

4

Click vidya-mentor-classes.apk

Tap on the downloaded file to open it.

5

Install

Tap the "Install" button on the screen that appears.

6

Scan now

If asked to scan the app, tap "Scan" and wait a moment for it to finish.

7

Open app

Once installed, tap "Open" and start learning.
